Introduction

Little Fighter Convergence is a LF2 modification made by Leviaiz (aka. Itari).
This mod features various characters from veteran modders around the world
blended with advanced data-changing techniques to provide refreshing play.

This modification features:

  • 21 heroes with new moveset and respective systems
  • 6 bosses
  • New stage mode
  • 21 handcrafted backgrounds

Balance
First things first, this mod is not intended to provide a 5:5 winrate among heroes, nor does it guarantee fair fights.
This mod aims to provide proper comeback for all heroes and suitable boss fights.
Keeping this in mind, heroes may more or less be similar in strength but may have extreme affinities.
I will look into this and make major changes in later releases .
  • Boss not requiring change: BanditEX, HongBa, Keny, Louis
  • Hero not requiring change: Deep, Firen, Freeze, Beltor, Cord, Nino, Pronto, Tiger
  • Hero requiring ultimate move: Brad, Caesar, Darius, Ryozaki, Winnie
  • Hero requiring major overhaul: Crimson, Mia, Rampe, Stancher, Wizard
